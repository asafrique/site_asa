In recent years, learning-based methods have demonstrated significant success in controlling complex cyber-physical systems (CPS). These techniques use learning-based tools either to derive mathematical models of a system for synthesizing controllers, or to directly learn the controller itself. However, applying learning-based methods in safety-critical CPS poses challenges, as these components are often regarded as black-box systems without formal guarantees. This presentation addresses how safety and performance guarantees can be recovered in that setting.
