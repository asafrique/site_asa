In this talk, I present a mathematical model with a standard incidence rate to assess the role of media such as Facebook, television, radio and Twitter in mitigating the outbreak of COVID-19. The basic reproduction number R0, the threshold parameter between disappearance and persistence of the disease, is computed and varies directly with the number of hospitalized people, asymptomatic and symptomatic carriers, and the impact of media coverage. Local and global stability are investigated using the Routh–Hurwitz criterion and Lyapunov's functional technique. A local sensitivity analysis assesses the impact of each model parameter on R0. The approximate rate at which herd immunity occurs under control measures is also computed, and numerical simulations based on available data corroborate the theoretical findings.
