We believe that the next revolution is quantum, involving the use of exponentially powerful computers called quantum computers. In these early days of such a revolution, quantum computers face crucial challenges, among which handling noise. To overcome this challenge, scientists are developing algorithms either to make the computers fault-tolerant or to correct where noise corrupts the information. In this talk, we introduce some of the quantum error correction codes and how they work.
