In this presentation, we study the problem of control of piezoelectric equations in a domain Ω. We use the electromagnetic wave to control the deformation of piezoelectric bodies through the current density j on the boundary Γ, the current density J in Ω, the electric charge density q in Ω, and h the surface force on ΓN. To construct those control laws, we use the a priori estimation technique.
